Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How are ceramic and fragile items packaged? ✦
All ceramic vessels, glass lamps, and marble sculptures are packed in high-density custom foam enclosures inside reinforced double-wall corrugated crates to guarantee damage-free transit.
What is your return policy? ✦
We offer a complimentary 30-day trial for all unused decorative items in their original packaging. Simply contact our concierge to schedule a white-glove courier return collection.
Do you offer trade discounts for interior architects? ✦
Yes, our Dream Trade Program offers exclusive tiered pricing, dedicated account managers, and custom lead-time coordination for licensed interior designers and architectural firms.
Are the brass and marble materials genuine? ✦
Absolutely. We use 100% solid unlacquered brass and natural quarry-mined Italian marble (such as Calacatta Viola and Nero Marquina). Every piece features unique organic stone veining.
